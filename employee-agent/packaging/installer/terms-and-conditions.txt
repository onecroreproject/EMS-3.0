1. Purpose and Scope of Monitoring
The Software is installed on company-authorized workstations to track working hours, measure productivity, verify attendance, and support transparent remote and hybrid work engagements.

2. Activity Tracking and Data Collection
During active work shifts, the Software collects the following metrics:
- Active Windows and Applications: Logs names and window titles of active applications and browser tabs used during work hours.
- Productivity and Idle Tracking: Monitors mouse and keyboard interaction levels to accurately measure active work time, idle time, and breaks.
- Automated Screen Capture: Takes periodic screenshots of your desktop display at scheduled intervals for work verification and compliance auditing.

3. Privacy Safeguards and Restrictions
The Company is committed to protecting employee privacy:
- No Keystroke Logging: The Software measures activity levels only and does not record individual keystrokes, passwords, or personal messages.
- No Audio or Webcam Access: The Software does not access, record, or stream your webcam or microphone.
- Secure Encrypted Transmission: All captured logs and screenshots are transmitted using industry-standard TLS encryption to protected company servers.
- Role-Based Access: Monitoring data is strictly restricted to authorized managerial, administrative, and HR personnel.

4. Shift-Based Operation
Monitoring operates solely during active work shifts. Employees retain control to pause monitoring during breaks or end tracking upon concluding their daily shift.
